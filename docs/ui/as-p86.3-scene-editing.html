Defaults when you switch how it plays. Now and then starts at every 10–20 s, Once at 0 s after entering — the same as a newly added effect. Switching back and forth on the same row keeps what you typed.
Allowed: decimals (1.5), 0 for Once (plays as you enter), equal numbers are not allowed for Now and then (every 15–15 s → red). Up to 600 s.
