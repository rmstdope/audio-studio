Make takes for every track not yet chosen (agreed)

On an act's Music page, the Tracks up to date panel offers Make 4 takes each for every loop and stinger with no chosen take that is not already making takes and that Setup's music model can make at its length. It asks first, inside the panel; Make starts four takes per track on Setup's music model, each row then shows its progress as when started by hand. While anything in the act is being made the panel says so and offers Stop all; when it is done a summary stays until Close or a reload.
