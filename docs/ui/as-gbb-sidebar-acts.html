Acts in the sidebar

Narration and Music are chosen alike: the open workspace lists its acts beneath it, the other is folded. Agreed in the design session for this change.

1 — On an act in Narration. Narration’s acts are listed, indented under it: “Act I” and the number of lines. Music’s acts are folded away. Only the open act is highlighted. The “Acts” list and its heading are gone.
