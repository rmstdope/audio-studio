Trim silence on a sound effect take

Agreed design: Trim silence at the left of the bottom row of every take card of an effect that plays once, and of the take in the game; Drop and Choose at the right. After trimming, the cut ends stay visible, hatched and dimmed; play plays only the part between; the length is the new one; a trimmed tag sits by the take's name; a line says how much was cut, in hundredths of a second, naming only an end that lost something. Undo trim gives back the take as made. On the take in the game, both rewrite the game's file at once.
