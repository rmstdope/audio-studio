A patch take's close-up shows its whole change

Each close-up frames what its own take keeps, together with the mark, plus a quarter of that size on each side, inside the picture. No close-up is more than one and a half times as tall as it is wide, or more than two and a half times as wide as it is tall: the frame is widened around the change to fit. A take that kept nothing, or is still being made, is framed on the mark as before.
