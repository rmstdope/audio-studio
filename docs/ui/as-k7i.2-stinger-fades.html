10 · What stays
Saved fades belong to the take. They're still there after a reload, they go with the take when it moves to another stinger, and they go when it's dropped. Lengths you haven't saved are lost on a reload or when you leave the page. Un-choosing a faded take works as it does today, and the take keeps its fades.
11 · How a take with fades plays
A take with fades of its own, whether on its card, in the editor or in the In the game block, plays without the playback fade from Setup, exactly as the game will play it. A take with no fades still gets the playback fade.
