Make takes for every stinger not yet chosen (agreed)

The Stingers page works as an act’s Music page does (docs/ui/as-23x.1-make-all.html), and says “stinger” throughout, including the panel heading “Stingers up to date” and the table’s “Stinger” column. The Stingers up to date panel offers Make 4 takes each for every stinger with no chosen take that is not already making takes. It asks first, inside the panel. While takes are being made, Refresh from the game is greyed, and while a refresh runs the panel’s button is greyed; each says why on hover. Every stinger fits every music model, so nothing is ever left out for its length.
