Move a take to another track (agreed)

A take that is not chosen can be moved to another loop in any act, or to another stinger. Chosen: a dialog to pick where; the take counts as made for the track it joins and keeps a "moved from" line for good; you stay where you were with a line saying where it went; with nowhere to move it, Move… is greyed and says why.

Where to move a take — Option A: a dialog

Each take card gets Move…. It opens a dialog listing every other loop in the game, by act, with what each one plays now and how long it is, so you can see where the take fits. Pick one, press Move take 3. Esc or Cancel closes it and nothing changes.

Moving a take — every state (for your check)

Built on the choices you made: the dialog, the take counts as made for the loop it joins, and you stay where you were with a line saying where it went.

1. The dialog, a loop selected

Focus starts in Find a loop. Up and down move through the list, Enter moves the take, Esc or Cancel closes it with nothing changed, and focus goes back to the take's Move… button. Move take 3 stays greyed until a loop is picked. Typing filters by name; nothing matching shows No loop is called that.
