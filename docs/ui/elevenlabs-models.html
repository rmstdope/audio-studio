Which model records each stage of the voices, and which makes the music. Saved in audio-studio.json for everyone on the project.

Those lines again, for the game you ship.

Same as draft: every line is recorded once, with no separate final recording.

Music (as before)

2 · A switch that puts recorded lines out of date

Switch ElevenLabs drafts to Flash v2.5?
  • ElevenLabs draft: Eleven v3 → Flash v2.5
  • 12 lines drafted with Eleven v3 become out of date: the next draft records them again, about 190 credits.
  • Flash v2.5 speaks cues aloud, so lines are sent without them; they stay in the cue files.
  • Saved in audio-studio.json; nothing else in it changes.

A switch with nothing at stake saves at once ("Saving…"); one that fails shows "Could not switch elevenlabs draft to Flash v2.5" with Keep and Try again, as Fish's rows do.
