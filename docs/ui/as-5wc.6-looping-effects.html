2 — A looping effect's page. The Length line says "10 s · loops · set by the game". Every looping take is exactly that length (never cut where the sound ends). ▶ plays it round until stopped; the orange mark at the end of the waveform is the seam; Hear the seam plays the last 4 s then the first 4 s, and reads "Stop" while it plays. The In the game block has the same.
