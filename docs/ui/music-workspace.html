Music — every state and word

The game lists the tracks; the studio makes them. A page per act and one for the stingers, takes as cards, the brief always editable. Every word here is what would ship.
Agreed: More takes makes 2 (about 6 minutes). Choosing another take switches the game's music at once, without asking: the take that was chosen stays on the board, so you can switch back. Takes from an earlier brief stay, marked "earlier brief". The chosen take has no Drop. Takes keep being made when you leave the page; the act's list shows "making 2 takes". Each act's page has Refresh from the game, the same as Refresh dialog on Narration, which brings both the lines and the loops; the Stingers page's runs the game's command for what belongs to no act. Stop cancels the waiting takes and lets the one under way finish. Statuses: chosen, not chosen, changed since chosen (what changed is said on the track's page), music file missing, making 2 takes. The kind (loop or stinger) is the game's alone. "Plays in" under a track's name is a line the game gives with the track.
