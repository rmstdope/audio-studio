Stable Audio Open — agreed

Stable Audio Open 1.0 and Small join the music models as Short and Shortest, under their own heading in every music menu (Setup’s, and the one beside More takes). They make short music only: a model that can’t make a track is greyed with what it can make and how long the track is. If Setup’s model can’t make a track, the track’s menu starts on it, says why, and waits for another pick. Setup’s checklist gets a Stable Audio row with three steps: accept the licence, make a token, download into ~/.cache/stable-audio. Times per take and the longest loop each model can make are examples, measured by whoever builds this.

Music generator (Stable Audio Open)Optional: for stingers and short loops. Its models are not on this computer.
  1. Sign in to Hugging Face and accept Stability AI’s Community Licence on stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0 and stabilityai/stable-audio-open-small.
  2. Make a read token at huggingface.co/settings/tokens.
  3. Download both into ~/.cache/stable-audio, where the studio looks for them:
    HF_TOKEN=… huggingface-cli download stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0 --local-dir ~/.cache/stable-audio/stable-audio-open-1.0
    HF_TOKEN=… huggingface-cli download stabilityai/stable-audio-open-small --local-dir ~/.cache/stable-audio/stable-audio-open-small
The studio never asks for or keeps your token.
