Option A — the bar always keeps the room for "Solo … · Clear", blank when nothing is soloed or muted

With nothing soloed or muted the row is empty space (the dotted outline is only in the drawing). Soloing fills it; Clear empties it. The bar's top (dashed) never moves. When everything fits on one row (a wide window, or a scene with no states or triggers), nothing is reserved and the count sits at the end of the row as today.
