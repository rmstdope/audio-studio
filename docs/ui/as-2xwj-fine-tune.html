Fine-tune the picture — agreed

A full-width panel “Fine-tune the picture” sits below Takes and above Named areas, the same way “Patches the game asks for” works: the picture in the game is shown large, you may drag an area on it, describe the change, and make 1 to 6 fine-tunes. Each fine-tune can be laid over the big picture to look at, used, or dropped.
