With no line at stake it saves at once ("Saving…"). One that fails shows "Could not save ElevenLabs' voice settings" with the reason, Keep the saved settings and Try again, as the model rows do. Escape is Keep. While a part records or a character auditions, the row is locked, as the other rows are.
