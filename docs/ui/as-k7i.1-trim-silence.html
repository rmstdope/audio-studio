Trim silence

Agreed design: Trim silence on every stinger take card, the take in the game included. After trimming, the cut start and end stay visible, hatched and dimmed; play plays only the part between; the length is the new one, a trimmed tag sits by the take's name and a line says how much was cut from each end. Undo trim gives back the take as made. On the take in the game, both rewrite the game's file at once.
