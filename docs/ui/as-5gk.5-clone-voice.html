Clone a voice from recordings on a character page (agreed)

Everything below is what a person would see and read. Words in quotes are the exact words that would ship.

With a voice already cast, the same button sits at the right of the panel’s play row (drawing A, 1b).

2 · Opened: focus lands in the name, which is the character’s name

Voice · clone from recordings
Makes a private voice on your Fish account from speech you have recorded. Only you can use it.
Drop audio files here, or
1 to 20 files, at least 10 seconds each. A minute or two in all gives the closest likeness.

Esc or Cancel closes it; focus returns to “Clone a voice from recordings”. Nothing was sent or written. Clone voice stays greyed out until there is a name, a recording long enough, and the tick.

4 · Cloning

Voice · clone from recordings
Sending 2 recordings to Fish and making the voice… this can take a minute.

No Cancel while cloning: once sent, Fish makes the voice either way. Leaving the page or reloading while cloning asks first: “The voice is still being made. If you leave, it is still made on your Fish account, but you won’t see it here.”

5 · Made: a sample of the new voice, focus on Cast

Voice · clone from recordings
Elior your voice
Made on your Fish account, private.

▶ plays the new voice saying the audition phrase, recorded by itself as for a cast voice. Cast asks first with the same confirmation as casting from the audition (“Cast Elior as Elior?”, listing what changes — recorded lines, an audition going on). When another voice is cast the button reads “Cast instead of Old Sage”. Bring into the audition puts it first among the voices to hear, starting an audition if none is going on, and goes to the audition. Close leaves the voice on your Fish account and Elior unchanged.

What is kept

A reload or leaving the page clears the form and its chosen files; nothing is saved by the studio until you cast or bring the voice into the audition. A voice once made stays on your Fish account.
