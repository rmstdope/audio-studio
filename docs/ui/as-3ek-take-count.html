How many takes to make

A count menu sits between the model menu and the button on a track's Takes panel, for the first batch and every later one. It lists 1 take to 6 takes and starts at 2 takes every time the panel opens and after every batch. The button says the number and, on a paid model, the total price. Screen readers call the menu “Number of new takes”.
