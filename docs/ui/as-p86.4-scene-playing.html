Playing a scene — every state and word

The play bar agreed in as-p86.1, now playing: the scene as the game plays it, a Now column saying what each row is doing, Mute and Solo for listening. Everything else on the page is as agreed in as-p86.1 and as-p86.3.

1. Stopped. No Now column. State can be set and is the state the scene starts in (here cat-home); Triggers are dimmed until it plays. M and S are on the music and every row and can be set before ▶. Nothing to play (no music, no effects): ▶ dimmed and “Nothing to play in this scene”, as agreed.

Hearing changes while it plays. A level is heard at once. A row whose How it plays, numbers or state change starts afresh as if just entered (a Now and then waits a new time; a Once waits its delay from now and can play again); an added row starts the same way; a removed row fades out over 0.3 s. Another music track crossfades over about a second, the effects playing on. Discard while playing plays on with the saved version, changed rows starting afresh. ■ fades everything out over 0.3 s. ▶ on a row (or the music) while the scene plays plays that clip once over the scene, at its level, whatever its M and S. A trigger fired while its row is for another state plays nothing.
Mute and Solo. M silences a row; S hears only soloed rows; solo wins over mute; each takes effect at once with the 0.3 s fade. The play bar shows “Muted: N”, “Solo: N”, or “Solo: N · Muted: N” and Clear (clears both); hidden when none. They are for listening only: never saved, never “Unsaved changes”. They last while you stay on the scene, through ■ and ▶, and are cleared by Clear, opening another scene, or a reload.
Keyboard. As agreed; in each row Tab reaches ▶, the row’s controls, M, S, then ✕. M and S are toggle buttons that say whether they are on. The Now column is read when focused, not announced as it changes. No page-wide shortcut.
Leaving. Opening another scene or page, Refresh from the game, or a reload stops playing; the play bar resets (state to the first, stopped) and Mute and Solo clear. While the leave dialog is open the scene plays on.
