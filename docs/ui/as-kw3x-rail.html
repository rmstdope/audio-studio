Collapsing the sidebar into an icon rail

Agreed: variant C (as-kw3x.1). A and B are kept for the record. Three ways to fold the left pane to a 72 px rail, icon over a small word. Each window is live: press the « / » button to fold and unfold, and click the rail. The page on the right is only a stand-in for the image page; its width tag shows what is won.
A · Rail with a flyout. Folded, the rail holds only the workspaces and the project pages. The acts of a workspace open in a panel that floats over the page when its icon is clicked, and close again when an act is picked or the pointer leaves.
  • Most room: the page keeps the full width all the time.
  • Changing act costs two clicks; the crumbs above the title also switch act.
  • Project name and credit move to a tip on the studio's mark.
B · Rail plus a lists column. The rail is always there. Beside it, a second column lists the open workspace's acts (and Style), with the project box at its foot. That column is what folds away; the rail never does.
  • Unfolded it is about as wide as today; folded it is just the rail.
  • Clicking the open workspace's icon again folds or unfolds its list.
  • Familiar from code editors; two columns to read when unfolded.
C · Rail with acts in it. Folded, the open workspace keeps its acts in the rail as short pills ("Act 1", "Act 2", "Style"), with a dot coloured by how much is done. Every page stays one click away.
  • No floating panels, nothing hidden.
  • Act names are shortened to fit; the full title is in the tip and the page title.
  • Counts become a coloured dot (green all done, amber some, grey none).
