What the game plays — agreed

On every track page (an act's loops and the stingers), what the game plays is shown on its own, in an "In the game" block between the Brief and the Takes panel: the chosen take, else the game's own file, else nothing. A chosen take can be un-chosen, after a question in the page; it goes back among the takes. The game's own file has no Un-choose.

1 — A take chosen over the game's own file. The chosen take is a green block of its own between Brief and Takes; Takes lists only the takes not chosen, in take-number order.
