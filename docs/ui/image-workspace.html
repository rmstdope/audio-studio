Image workspace: three layouts

Today an image's page is a column: the prompt at the top, then Takes with Make at its head, two large pictures (each up to the game's size × its whole zoom), Drop and Choose under the right one, and the strip of the other takes below them. Making, picking, choosing and dropping each sit at a different height, so working one image means scrolling up and down. Each layout below keeps the whole loop (edit words → make → look → choose or drop) in one window, 1240 × 760 here.
  • The pictures are pixel-art stand-ins; press thumbs, Choose, Drop and Make to try the flow.
  • The blue numbers mark what each note below the frame talks about.
The frame is a 1240 × 760 window; nothing in it needs the page to scroll.
