Designing a voice with ElevenLabs (14.12)

Design a voice… opens the same box, on the board and on the character's page, with a switch at the top. ElevenLabs makes 3 voices; each preview says the audition phrase, then the character's own lines until ElevenLabs' least of 100 characters (or a text ElevenLabs writes, when she has too few). It costs a credit a character of that text, once.
