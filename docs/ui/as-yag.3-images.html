Images — every state and word

The game lists its images and style; the studio keeps your own words for them. Every word here is what would ship.

Agreed: Images is a workspace beside Narration and Music; it opens into the acts and one Style page. Each act's page lists its images (Image, Prompt, Size, Status); pressing one opens its own page, where its words are edited and, later, its takes are made. Your words save as you leave the field, are marked “changed here” with “use the game's”, and never reach the game; an empty field or the game's words typed back is the same as use the game's. The Style page: the look and what is kept out can be changed (marked the same way); the palette and each act's addition are the game's. Without a style file each prompt is its own words only. A changed game description under your words: compare and choose (Keep mine / Use the game's), and the act's row says so until you choose. A changed game style: one notice on every Images page until Seen, remembered across reloads, back on the next change; out-of-date marks stay. Statuses: none yet, chosen, out of date (the image's page says what changed).
