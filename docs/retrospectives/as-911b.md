## A music test failed in CI on a docs-only change

**What happened.** CI failed on `src/services/musicTakes.test.ts` › "the summary of a press on a list is kept by the studio > goes on while takes are started by hand during the press, and a new press replaces it" (44.89 ms). The same test passed three times alone and in the full local `bun run check`; a bare re-run of the failed job passed.

**Why.** Not established; the test looks timing-dependent, and the diff touched only a skill file.

**Cost.** One re-run (about 3 minutes) of the budget of two.

**Prevent by.** Giving that test a deterministic wait instead of real timing, in its own bead.

**Seen before.** Nothing like it yet.
